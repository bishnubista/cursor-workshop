# Codebase Exploration Guide

This branch helps you practice exploring codebases with @codebase.

## Sample Project for Exploration

This workshop repository itself is a good codebase to explore!

Try these @codebase queries:

1. "What branches exist and what do they teach?"
2. "Find all TASK.md files and summarize them"
3. "What JavaScript patterns are demonstrated?"
4. "List all exercises across all branches"
5. "Find common themes in BRANCH_NOTES files"

## Practice on Your Own Projects

After this workshop:
1. Open one of your projects in Cursor
2. Use @codebase to explore it
3. Ask architectural questions
4. Find code patterns
5. Discover areas for improvement

The more you practice, the better @codebase becomes at understanding your code!
